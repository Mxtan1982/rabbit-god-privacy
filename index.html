Rabbit God: Unfinished Love is a multilingual novel reading application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, processed, stored, and shared when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require users to create an account and we do not directly ask users to provide their name, email address, phone number, home address, password, or other account information.

The application may save certain preferences locally on the user's device, including:

  • Reading progress
  • Selected language
  • Font size
  • Reading theme or night mode preference
  • Bookmarked chapters
  • Advertising-related reading counters or reward status

This local information is primarily stored on the user's device and is used to provide reading features and remember user preferences.

2. Advertising

This application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements, and rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process certain information when advertisements are requested or displayed. Depending on the user's device, region, consent choices, and Google services configuration, this information may include:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Advertising interactions
  • Ad impressions and clicks
  • App performance information
  • Diagnostic information
  • Consent and privacy choices

This information may be used by Google and its partners to provide, measure, personalize where permitted, prevent fraud, improve, and report advertising services.

We do not directly control how Google processes information collected through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Users should also review Google's applicable privacy and advertising policies.

3. Consent Management

The application may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) or other consent mechanisms where required.

Depending on the user's location and applicable law, users may be shown privacy or consent choices before certain advertising technologies are used.

Advertising behavior may differ depending on the consent choices made by the user.

4. How We Use Information

Information handled by the application may be used to:

  • Remember reading progress
  • Remember language and display preferences
  • Provide bookmark functionality
  • Improve the reading experience
  • Display advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Maintain application security and stability

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information ourselves.

Information may be processed or shared with third-party service providers that are necessary to operate advertising and application functionality.

Our primary third-party advertising provider is:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK

Third-party providers process information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

6. Data Storage

Reading progress, bookmarks, selected language, font preferences, and similar application settings may be stored locally on the user's device.

Users may remove locally stored application information by clearing the application's storage/data through their device settings or by uninstalling the application.

7. Data Retention

Locally stored reading preferences remain on the user's device until they are cleared, overwritten, or the application is uninstalled.

Data processed by Google AdMob or other third-party services is subject to the retention practices of those service providers.

8. Children's Privacy

Rabbit God: Unfinished Love is a novel reading application intended for a general adult audience and is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly request children to submit personal information directly through the application.

Advertising and audience settings may also be configured according to the application's Google Play audience classification and applicable Google policies.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to limit unnecessary data collection and to protect application functionality.

However, no electronic transmission, advertising service, software system, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. International Users

The application is available to users in multiple countries and languages. Third-party services such as Google AdMob may process information on servers located outside the user's country or region.

Such processing is handled in accordance with the policies and legal obligations of the relevant service provider.

11. Your Choices

Depending on your device, region, and applicable laws, you may have options to:

  • Change advertising privacy choices
  • Reset or limit your device advertising identifier
  • Clear locally stored application data
  • Uninstall the application
  • Manage privacy settings provided by Google or Android

12. Third-Party Services

Rabbit God: Unfinished Love may use third-party libraries and services, including Google AdMob and Google Mobile Ads.

These third-party services may collect and process information independently according to their own privacy policies.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, advertising services, legal requirements, or data practices change.
